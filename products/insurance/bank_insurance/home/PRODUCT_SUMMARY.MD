## Product Overview

This is a comprehensive home insurance product designed to protect homeowners and renters in Belgium. The insurance covers material damage to insured property owned by the insured or for which they are responsible as a tenant or user (tenant liability).

### Target Market
- Primary residences (owner-occupied)
- Rental properties (landlords)
- Tenants/renters
- Properties with professional use (freelance/office, excluding pharmacies)
- Private garages

## Coverage Details

### BASE COVERAGES (Included)

#### 1. **Fire and Related Perils**
- Fire damage
- Sudden abnormal smoke or soot development
- Scorching
- Explosion or implosion
- Lightning and electrical damage to appliances/installations
- Electrocution of pets

#### 2. **Weather-Related Damage**
- Storm, hail, snow and ice pressure
- Coverage for building and contents
- Exclusions: Outdoor items (except garden furniture and barbecues), gardens (can be covered by Garden Option)

#### 3. **Water Damage**
- Damage from hydraulic installations and heating systems
- Coverage includes:
  - Leaks from pipes, appliances, sanitary installations
  - Overflow from neighboring properties
  - Defrosting damage to food items
- Exclusions:
  - Condensation
  - Groundwater
  - Rainwater infiltration through walls or chimneys
  - Damage from construction/renovation work (can be covered by Works Option)
  - Public sewer backup/flooding (covered under Natural Disasters)

#### 4. **Fuel Oil Damage**
- Coverage for accidental fuel oil leaks
- Includes value of accidentally leaked fuel oil
- Exclusions: Tanks not complying with regulations, soil remediation (covered by Garden Option)

#### 5. **Glass Breakage**
- Breaking or cracking of windows and related materials
- Coverage for opacity of insulating glazing after supplier warranty expires
- Exclusions: Non-fixed glass, items during transportation, scratches

#### 6. **Theft-Related Damage to Building**
- Damage to immovable property from theft, attempted theft or vandalism
- Only if building is inhabited
- Coverage for graves against vandalism (max €5,000)
- Coverage for tenant/occupier liability to landlord

#### 7. **Impact and Collision**
- Damage from objects, trees, animals
- Damage from third-party immovable property

#### 8. **Civil Liability - Building and Contents**
Coverage up to:
- **€28,935,590.24** for bodily injury
- **€3,616,888.93** for material and immaterial damage

Covers:
- Liability for damage caused by the building, sidewalks, gardens, swimming pools
- Stored contents on these locations
- Failure to clear ice/snow from sidewalks
- Abnormal neighborhood nuisance (Article 3.101 Belgian Civil Code) if caused by accident

#### 9. **Natural Disasters**
Coverage for:
- Earthquakes (magnitude 4+ on Richter scale or causing damage within 10km radius)
- Landslides or ground subsidence
- Flooding from watercourses, canals, lakes, ponds, seas
- Public sewer backup or saturation

Specific limits apply per Article 130 §2 of the Insurance Law of April 4, 2014.

Exclusions:
- Items located outside buildings (can be covered by Garden Option)
- Buildings under construction/renovation (can be covered by Works Option)
- Goods in transport

#### 10. **Labor Conflicts and Attacks**
- Direct damage from persons participating in labor conflicts or attacks (excluding terrorism)
- Damage from measures by authorities to protect insured property
- Limited to €1,651,962.49 (indexed to ABEX 833)

#### 11. **Terrorism**
- Coverage according to Belgian Terrorism Insurance Law (May 3, 2024)
- Annual limit of €1.7 billion (indexed) shared among all Belgian insurers
- For residential properties: no sublimit per location

#### 12. **Home Assistance (24/7)**

**Without Claim:**
- Emergency interventions for sudden, unforeseen events
- Coverage up to €310 per intervention (max €40 for materials)
- Limited to 3 interventions per calendar year
- Response within 2 hours, on-site within 24 hours
- Covered situations:
  - Water/fuel oil leaks
  - Lockout situations
  - Lost/stolen keys
  - Urgent interventions to prevent damage

Exclusions: Replacement of appliances, meter issues, maintenance work, clogged pipes, power/water cuts by supplier

**With Claim:**
- Leak detection services
- Locksmith assistance (max €260)
- Emergency protective measures
- Temporary accommodation assistance
- Child/pet care (max €400) if hospitalized 24+ hours
- Repatriation costs if presence required

### OPTIONAL COVERAGES

#### 1. **Theft Option**
Covers theft and vandalism of contents up to 50% of insured contents value.

**Coverage includes:**
- Theft/attempted theft from the insured building
- Vandalism
- Theft with violence/threats
- Theft from temporarily relocated contents (with break-in)
- Theft from student accommodation (with break-in in private area)
- Garden furniture and barbecues located outside

**Limits:**
- All jewelry: €10,000
- Temporarily relocated contents (with break-in): €7,500
- Student accommodation contents: €7,500
- All outbuildings contents: €7,500
- Theft with violence/threats: €7,500
- All securities: €2,500
- Garden furniture and barbecues: €2,500
- Entrusted goods/guest belongings: €2,500
- Theft by authorized persons: €2,500 (claim within 24 hours required)

**Additional coverage:**
- Lock replacement costs after key theft

**Exclusions:**
- Animals
- Vehicles subject to mandatory liability insurance (except motorized wheelchairs)
- Construction materials (can be covered by Works Option)
- Contents outside buildings (except explicitly mentioned)
- Common areas in apartments if not locked
- Private garages at different addresses
- Nursing homes

#### 2. **English Franchise**
- Zero deductible if total damage exceeds €298 (indexed)

#### 3. **Indirect Losses**
- 10% increase in compensation after deductible
- Limited to €12,000 per claim
- Excludes: Liability coverages, Theft, Home Assistance, Legal Protection, expertise costs

#### 4. **Legal Protection - Building**

**Coverage limits (not indexed):**
- €30,000 total: Criminal defense, civil recourse, civil defense
- €7,500: Contractual disputes
- €7,500: Insolvency of liable parties

**Covered situations:**
- Criminal defense after covered claim
- Civil recourse for damages from third parties
- Contractual disputes (consumer protection, construction follow-up damage, rental disputes)
- Insolvency recovery

**Free choice of lawyer and expert**

**Waiting period:** 3 months for contractual legal protection

**Exclusions:**
- Fines and penalties
- Bank/credit card theft and electronic payment fraud
- Vehicle theft (if not at risk address or locked garage)

#### 5. **Waiver of Recourse**
- Insurer waives recourse against tenant/occupier
- Also applies to rented contents

#### 6. **Temporary Waiver of Recourse**
- Applies to temporary tenants/occupiers

#### 7. **Garden Option**
Coverage up to €5,000 per claim without proportionality rule.

**Covers:**
- Plantings and ponds
- Garden structures (not anchored in concrete/walls/foundations)
- Garden furniture, accessories, tools, decorative elements
- Play equipment, barbecues, patio heaters, outdoor kitchens
- Contents located outside (max €2,500 for storm/hail/snow/natural disasters)

**Specific coverages:**
- Garden damage from covered claims/natural disasters/wild animals
- Garden contents outside (max €5,000)
- Theft of plantings and garden contents (max €5,000) if Theft option subscribed
- Soil remediation from fuel oil (max €10,000)
- Leak detection and ground excavation costs

**Exclusions:** Swimming pools, swim ponds, outdoor jacuzzis (covered by Pool Option)

#### 8. **Pool Option**
Coverage up to €15,000 without proportionality rule.

**Covers:**
- Indoor and outdoor pools
- Outdoor jacuzzis and swim ponds
- Permanently anchored or excavated in property
- Pool accessories (liner, cover, solar collectors, heating, filtration, pumps, lighting, jets)
- Pool maintenance materials and equipment

**Specific coverages:**
- Material damage from covered claims/natural disasters/wild animals
- Theft and vandalism
- Pool overflow/leakage damage
- Water replacement after contamination or leakage (once per claim/year)

**Exclusions:** Natural ponds (covered by Garden Option), frost damage, corrosion, progressive damage, lack of maintenance

#### 9. **Parked Vehicle Option**
Coverage up to €15,000 at sales value for vehicles with 4+ wheels and 50cc+ capacity, trailers, caravans, pleasure boats.

**Covered perils:**
- Fire, lightning, smoke, explosion, impact, storm, hail, snow, labor conflicts, attacks (if building also affected)
- Natural disasters (if inside building and building affected)

**Exclusions:**
- Damage covered by mandatory vehicle insurance
- Theft/vandalism of vehicle/contents (contents theft covered if vehicle inside building with Theft option)
- Temporarily relocated vehicles
- Collision with other vehicles/animals
- Impact with poles/trees (except storm-related)
- Electrical malfunction
- Glass breakage (except from covered peril)

#### 10. **Media Option**
Coverage up to €2,000 per claim without proportionality rule.

**Covers:**
- Electronic devices with computer/multimedia functions
- Laptops, tablets, LED TVs, Smart TVs (minimum 19-inch screen)
- For private and professional use
- Phones primarily for telephony: excluded

**Coverage:**
- Material damage not explicitly covered by base coverages
- Data recovery/restoration costs for lost data
- Software replacement costs or licenses after device replacement
- Theft: NOT covered by this option

**Exclusions:**
- Normal wear and tear
- Material/construction/assembly defects or non-compliant use
- Devices not in building at time of claim (except student accommodations, vacation homes, second residences)
- Cybercrime/hacking
- Electromagnetic fields (except lightning)
- Damage under manufacturer/supplier/repairer warranty

#### 11. **Business Option**
Coverage up to €15,000 without proportionality rule for professional contents related to freelance/office activities.

**Covers:**
- Equipment, inventory, securities related to professional activity
- Extended base coverages to professional contents
- Storm/hail coverage for signs and billboards
- Glass breakage for illuminated signs
- Water damage from automatic sprinkler systems
- Theft and vandalism of professional contents (if Theft option subscribed)
- Theft of securities up to €5,000 for theft with violence/threats
- Reimbursement of counterfeit banknotes accepted in good faith
- Temporarily relocated contents for exhibitions/trade shows in EU (max €5,000)

**Operating loss coverage:**
- €100/day for complete business interruption
- Maximum 30 days
- Minimum 1 week interruption from claim date

**Valuation:**
- Equipment: Replacement value (first 5 years), actual value thereafter
- IT/multimedia equipment: Replacement value (first 2 years), actual value thereafter
- Inventory and securities: Current value
- Tenant improvements: Replacement value

**Exclusions:**
- Bank/credit card theft and electronic payment fraud
- Theft from vehicles not at risk address or locked garage
- Loss/destruction from authority measures (except protective measures)
- Temperature changes not from covered claim

#### 12. **Works Option**
Coverage up to €10,000 without proportionality rule for construction/renovation work.

**Requirements:**
- Work performed by registered contractor
- No structural impact on building
- Coverage duration: as specified in special conditions

**Covers:**
- Water damage and fuel oil leaks caused by work
- Theft of construction materials and technical installations, vandalism
- Natural disasters for buildings under construction (if uninhabitable due to work)
- All-risk coverage for building damage caused by work
- Accident coverage for medical costs of insured and visitors

**No deductible for this option**

**Exclusions:**
- Self-performed work
- Maintenance work
- Design/calculation/plan errors without material consequential damage
- Equipment/tool breakage
- Immaterial losses
- Partial/total work abandonment
- Non-compliance with regulations/technical standards/safety rules

#### 13. **Tenant Protection Option**

**Covers:**
- Rental disputes with landlord (max €7,500, 3-month waiting period)
- Moving accident medical costs for volunteer helpers (max €2,500, no deductible)
- Financial assistance for job loss (€1,000 flat rate, 3-month waiting period)
- Moving allowance for forced relocation (€500 flat rate, no deductible)

**Forced relocation reasons:**
- Death, accident causing permanent disability
- Divorce or end of legal cohabitation
- Early termination by landlord

**Exclusions:**
- Disputes over non-payment of rent

## General Conditions and Requirements

### Property Requirements

**Eligible Properties:**
- Located in Belgium
- Primary residences (owner-occupied or rented)
- Properties with freelance/office use (pharmacies excluded)
- Private garages

**Property Types:**
- Houses (2, 3, or 4 facades)
- Apartments
- Apartment buildings
- Mobile homes (static caravans)

**Property Condition:**
- Inhabited properties
- Uninhabited properties (limited coverage, especially theft)
- Properties undergoing work (requires Works Option for full coverage)
- Properties over 30% dilapidated: excluded

### Insured Parties

**Insured persons include:**
- Policyholder
- Persons living with policyholder (even if temporarily elsewhere)
- Personnel and dependents in performance of duties
- Authorized representatives and partners in performance of duties
- Guests (limited coverage: max €5,000 for contents)

### Valuation Methods

**Building:**
- **Owners:** New replacement value
- **Tenants/Occupiers:** Actual value (tenant liability coverage)

**Contents:**
| Item Type | Valuation Method |
|-----------|------------------|
| Linen and clothing | Actual value |
| Antique furniture, art, collectibles, jewelry, precious metal items | Replacement value |
| Securities | Current value |
| Pets | Current value (excl. emotional/competition value) |
| Garden tools, bicycles, motorized vehicles <50cc | Actual value |
| Professional equipment | Actual value, not exceeding new replacement value for comparable equipment |
| Documents, plans, models, data files | Material reconstruction cost |
| Inventory | Production cost |
| Client goods or entrusted goods | Actual value |

**Most contents:** New replacement value

### Insurance Period

**Contract duration:** 1 year, automatically renewed for successive 1-year periods

**Start date:** Date specified in special conditions after:
- Both parties sign the policy
- Payment of first premium

### Deductibles

**Standard deductible:** €265.42 (indexed with consumer price index, base January 2020 = 109.69)

**Applied to:** Each claim (all damage from single event = one claim)

**Exceptions (no deductible):**
- Building damage if policyholder chooses in-kind restoration
- English Franchise option (if damage > €298)
- Home Assistance
- Works Option
- Legal Protection
- Tenant Protection Option (specific coverages)

### Coverage Limits

**Indexation:**
- Building and contents sums insured: ABEX index
- Liability limits, Home Assistance, franchises: Consumer price index
- Natural disasters limits: Not indexed
- Optional coverages (Legal Protection, Garden, Pool, Parked Vehicle, Media, Business, Works, Tenant): Not indexed

**Liability limits (indexed):**
- Bodily injury: €28,935,590.24
- Material and immaterial damage: €3,616,888.93

**Other limits:** As specified per coverage/option

### Territorial Scope

**Primary coverage:** Address specified in special conditions (Belgium)

**Extensions:**
- Tenant/occupier liability: Worldwide for temporary residences, student accommodation, event halls (max €2,500,000)
- Private garage at different Belgian address
- Contents temporarily relocated to other building (100% of contents sum insured, worldwide)
- Nursing home (for contents of direct ascendants)
- Moving period (90 days for both old and new address, 30 days for Theft option)

**Not covered:** Second residences (requires separate policy)

### Exclusions (General)

**Never covered:**
1. Seizure/occupation by military or police forces
2. Nuclear damage, radioactivity, ionizing radiation
3. War or similar events, civil war
4. Additional damage from loss/theft after claim
5. Claims intentionally caused by insured
6. Damage covered by supplier/manufacturer/repairer/lessor warranty
7. Damage existing before contract start
8. Time-barred claims (3 years per Belgian Insurance Law)
9. Repeat claims from same unresolved cause
10. Damage to structures in decay (>30% dilapidated) or destined for demolition

## Claims Procedures

### Reporting Requirements

**Time limits:**
- Standard claims: Within 8 days
- Animal damage: Immediately
- Theft/attempted theft/vandalism: Immediately (file police report within 24 hours)
- Labor conflicts/attacks: Take all steps with authorities ASAP for compensation

**Required information:**
- Policy number
- Damage description (cause, date, time, damaged items, any injuries)
- Police report if applicable
- Other involved parties (witnesses, other damaged parties, liable parties)
- Other insurance policies covering the same damage
- Photos (as many as possible)

**Methods:**
- Via Belfius Mobile app (myBo virtual assistant)
- Online at belfius.be/schade
- Phone: +32 (0)2 286 70 00 (Mon-Fri 8am-5pm)

### Insured's Obligations

1. **Prevent and limit damage:**
   - Take all necessary measures before and during claim
   - Don't make unauthorized changes that hinder damage assessment

2. **If liability claimed:**
   - Don't admit liability (first aid/acknowledging facts OK)
   - Don't make payments or promises
   - Immediately provide all legal and extra-legal documents
   - Appear at hearings if requested

3. **Notify insurer of:**
   - Other insurance policies covering same damage
   - Waiver of recourse to liable parties (except specified situations)

**Consequences of non-compliance:**
- Fraudulent intent: Coverage denial possible
- Other cases: Compensation reduction proportional to insurer's prejudice

### Claims Settlement

**Compensation payment timeline:**

1. **Accommodation costs and urgent needs:** Within 15 days of proof of expenses

2. **Undisputed compensation amount:** Within 30 days of agreement

3. **Disputed amount:** Within 30 days after expertise completion or damage assessment
   - Expertise must be completed within 90 days of claim notification
   - If insured appoints expert: 90 days starts from notification of expert appointment

**Timeline suspensions:**
- If insured hasn't fulfilled all contractual obligations
- Criminal investigation: Insurer may request access to criminal file (30-day deadline applies after receiving conclusions)
- Reasons beyond insurer's control

**Advance payments available** for urgent costs if building uninhabitable

**Interest on late payments:** Double the legal interest rate

### Valuation of Damage

**Property valuation:** Based on value at date of claim

**Depreciation:**
- New replacement value: Depreciation only applied if >30% of new value
- No depreciation for electrical/electronic devices

**Tenant liability damage:** Actual value

**Disagreement resolution:**
- Two experts (one per party)
- Third expert if needed (majority vote)
- Insurer advances costs for insured's expert and third expert
- Costs borne by party in wrong

### Compensation Limits

**Minimum compensation for covered claims:**
- **Building:** 100% of compensation excluding VAT, after deduction of depreciation >30%
- **Contents:** 100% of compensation including VAT
- Indexed amount limited to 120% of initially determined compensation

**Expertise costs:**
Covered according to scale (min €331.25, max €27,535.28 per claim):

| Compensation (excl. VAT) | Fee Rate |
|--------------------------|----------|
| Up to €16,562.58 | 5.00% |
| €16,562.58 to €82,812.87 | 3.00% |
| €82,812.87 to €248,438.60 | 1.75% |
| €248,438.60 to €496,877.20 | 1.50% |
| €496,877.20 to €1,656,257.31 | 0.75% |
| Above €1,656,257.31 | 0.35% |

Percentages applied per bracket.

**Additional costs covered:**
- Leak detection costs
- Rescue costs
- Extinguishing and preservation costs
- Debris removal and demolition
- Garden restoration after rescue operations
- Accommodation costs during reconstruction
- Loss of enjoyment/rental income
- Medical costs (max €3,000/victim for 365 days post-claim)
- Funeral costs (max €4,000/victim)
- Civil recourse costs
- New building code compliance costs (regulated increases only, excluding prior obligations or illegal constructions)

### Recovered Property

**If compensation not yet paid:** Only material damage to recovered items compensated

**If compensation already paid:** Insured chooses:
- Return items and refund compensation (minus damage to items) within 60 days, OR
- Give recovered items to insurer and keep compensation

**At inception:**
- Answer all questions truthfully
- Provide all information reasonably relevant to risk assessment

**During contract:**
- Report all changes with significant and lasting impact on risk

**Consequences of unintentional misrepresentation:**
- Insurer proposes adjustment within 1 month of discovery
- If insurer would never have accepted risk: May cancel within 1 month
- If claim before adjustment: Compensation reduced proportionally (if no fraud)

**Consequences of intentional misrepresentation:**
- Contract void
- Paid premiums forfeited

**Risk reduction:**
- If significant lasting risk reduction: Premium reduction granted
- If no agreement within 1 month: Policyholder may cancel

### Policy Modifications

**Insurer must be notified of:**
- Building/contents changes
- Ownership/occupancy changes
- Address changes

### Cancellation Rights

**Policyholder may cancel:**
- At least 2 months before annual renewal
- After first year: Anytime with 2 months' notice
- If contract signature to inception >1 year: Up to 3 months before inception
- After rate change (subject to conditions)
- After partial coverage cancellation by insurer
- After any claim: Within 1 month of payment/refusal
- For risk reduction if no agreement
- **Distance contracts: 14 calendar days cooling-off period** (pro-rated premium if coverage started)

**Insurer may cancel:**
- At least 3 months before annual renewal
- If contract signature to inception >1 year: Up to 3 months before inception
- After claim: Within 1 month of payment/refusal
- For non-payment of premium
- For unintentional misrepresentation
- For risk increase
- If insured refuses required preventive measures

**Cancellation methods:**
- Registered mail

**Automatic termination:**
