# Funeral Insurance (Uitvaartverzekering) - Product Summary

## Product Overview

**Provider:** Belfius Direct Verzekeringen (Belfius Insurance NV)
**Product Type:** Branch 21 Life Insurance (lifelong coverage)
**Reference:** FIF L49-15012024
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2024

## General Requirements

### Eligibility

- **Policyholder:** Must be over 18 years old
- **Insured Person:** Can be up to 99 years old at contract inception
- **Residency:** Both policyholder and insured must be domiciled in Belgium at contract inception
- **Medical Requirements:** No medical examination required

### Contract Types

The policyholder can choose between:
1. **Single Premium:** One-time payment (available up to age 99 of insured)
2. **Periodic Premiums:** Payments over 10, 15, or 20 years (available up to age 75 of insured)
   - For distance contracts, the period is limited to 15 years

### Payment Frequencies

- Annual
- Semi-annual (2% fractionation cost)
- Quarterly (3% fractionation cost)
- Monthly (4% fractionation cost)

## Coverage Details

### Main Death Benefit Coverage

**Insured Capital:** Between €2,250 and €10,000 (chosen by policyholder)

#### Waiting Period for Periodic Premium Contracts

For death by natural causes or illness within the first 2 years:

| Period After Inception | Benefit Paid |
|------------------------|--------------|
| First 6 months | Refund of paid premiums (excluding taxes) |
| 6-12 months | 25% of insured capital |
| 12-18 months | 50% of insured capital |
| 18-24 months | 75% of insured capital |
| After 24 months | 100% of insured capital |

#### Waiting Period for Single Premium Contracts

- **First 6 months:** Refund of paid premium (less taxes)
- **After 6 months:** 100% of insured capital

#### Accidental Death

- **100% of insured capital** paid from the first day of contract inception (no waiting period)

### Children's Coverage

**Automatic Coverage:** Children of the insured (aged 6 months to under 18 years) living with parents/guardians are automatically covered.

**Benefit:** Up to actual funeral costs, maximum:
- Insured capital amount of parent(s)' contract
- Capped at €2,500 per child

**Limitations:**
- Same child can only be covered by one funeral insurance policy
- Same waiting period restrictions apply as for main insured
- No surrender or conversion value for children's coverage

### Assistance Coverage

The policy includes assistance benefits for the spouse and children of the insured residing at the same Belgian address:

#### Immediate Assistance in Belgium (within 30 days of death)

1. **Childcare:** Up to 5 consecutive days for children under 16 and/or disabled children
2. **Pet Care:** Accommodation for dogs and cats for up to 5 consecutive days
3. **Household Help:** Up to 16 hours over maximum 5 consecutive days

#### Psychological Assistance (within 180 days of death)

- Up to 5 sessions with recognized psychologist
- Sessions in Belgium or by phone if abroad

#### Repatriation Services

- Transportation of remains from abroad to Belgium
- Funeral treatment and coffin costs (up to €1,500)
- Return travel organization for family members abroad

#### Care Manager Support (within 30 days of death)

- Administrative assistance (bank, post office, insurance, pensions, etc.)
- Organization of services (housework, meal delivery, personal transport, etc.)
- Limited to 20 hours and 6 interventions per case
- **Note:** Service costs remain beneficiary's responsibility

#### Information Service

Access to information about:
- Government agencies for wills, guardianship, inheritance tax
- Required formalities and contacts (banks, employers, health insurance)
- Medical and paramedical services

### Indexation Option

If offered to the policyholder, insured capital can be indexed according to conditions in the insurance offer and Special Conditions.

## Premium Calculation Information

### Segmentation Criteria

The premium is determined based on the following criteria:

#### 1. Age of Insured (Acceptance & Tarification)

- **Primary factor** in risk assessment
- Younger age at subscription = lower premium
- Death risk increases with age
- Premium remains **unchanged** during premium payment period

#### 2. Address of Insured (Acceptance Criterion)

- Insured must be domiciled in Belgium

#### 3. Address of Policyholder (Acceptance Criterion)

- Policyholder must be domiciled in Belgium
- For legal entities: registered office must be in Belgium

#### 4. Date and Cause of Death (Coverage Scope)

- **Date of death:** Determines if death occurred during waiting period
- **Cause of death:** Natural death, accident, or illness
- Both factors influence benefit amount payable

## Costs and Fees

### Taxes

- **Individuals:** 2% insurance tax on premiums
- **Legal Entities:** 4.4% insurance tax on premiums

### Surrender Costs

- **5% of theoretical surrender value** on surrender request date
- Reduced by 1% per year from policyholder's 60th birthday
- **0% at age 65**
- **Minimum surrender cost:** €60 (indexed to health consumer price index)

### Conversion Costs

- **0.5% of current value** of outstanding premiums

### Minimum Values

- Minimum surrender value: €30 (indexed)
- Minimum theoretical surrender value for partial/full surrender: €30 (indexed)
- Minimum theoretical surrender value for conversion: €120 (indexed)

## Exclusions

### Not Covered:

1. **Suicide** within 12 months of:
   - Contract inception
   - Contract reinstatement
   - Capital increase (exclusion applies only to increased amount)
   - **Exception:** Euthanasia performed in Belgium according to legal requirements IS covered

2. **Intentional Acts:**
   - Death caused intentionally by policyholder or beneficiary
   - Death as direct result of intentional crime/misdemeanor by insured, policyholder, or beneficiary

3. **Riots:** Death due to voluntary active participation in riots, civil unrest, or collective violence

4. **War:**
   - Death from war actions or military hostilities
   - Death during active participation in hostilities
   - Death while traveling to countries under armed conflict with negative travel advisory from Ministry of Foreign Affairs

5. **Nuclear Accidents:**
   - Direct/indirect exposure to ionizing radiation
   - Nuclear activities
   - Weapons designed to explode through atomic structure change

6. **Terrorism:** Covered according to Law of May 3, 2024, with limitations:
   - Limited to €1.7 billion per calendar year for all events
   - Amount indexed annually based on consumer price index
   - Proportional reduction if total claims exceed annual limit

### Assistance Exclusions:

- Natural disasters or nuclear accidents
- Terrorism incidents
- Motorized events (races, rallies) when insured participates
- Countries without infrastructure
- Death due to alcohol/drug use
- Countries in civil/foreign war or with security disruptions
- Countries with general travel ban or entry prohibition
- Countries under international sanctions

## Contract Duration and Termination

### Duration

The contract lasts for the lifetime of the insured.

### Automatic Termination Upon:

1. Death of insured
2. Full surrender of policy
3. Non-payment of premiums (after 30-day notice period)
4. Cancellation within 30 days of contract inception

### Right of Withdrawal

- **In-office contracts:** 30 days from inception date
- **Distance contracts:** 30 days from contract conclusion or receipt of all contract conditions (whichever is later)
