## Coverage Details

### 1. Civil Liability Coverage (Burgerlijke Aansprakelijkheid)

#### **Coverage Limits:**
- **Bodily Injury (Lichamelijk Letsel):** €26,576,605 per claim (indexed)
  - Base index value: €31,215,521.56 (December 2022)
- **Material Damage (Materiële Schade):** €5,315,321 per claim (indexed)
  - Base index value: €6,243,104.31 (December 2022)

#### **What is Covered:**

**General Coverage:**
- Extra-contractual civil liability for damage caused to third parties in private life
- Damage covered under Belgian Civil Code articles 6.5, 6.10, 6.11, 6.12, 6.13, 6.14, 6.16, and 6.17
- Excessive neighborhood nuisance (article 3.101) when caused by an accident
- Objective liability for fire and explosion (Law of July 30, 1979)
- Objective liability for weak road users (article 29bis of November 21, 1989 law)
- Volunteer work activities (as defined by July 3, 2005 law)

**Animals:**
- Damage caused by household pets owned by or under care of insured persons
- Damage caused by poultry kept without profit motive
- Damage caused by dogs used occasionally for business premises security
- Damage caused by rented or borrowed riding horses (includes contractual liability up to €6,000 non-indexed per claim)

**Transportation & Vehicles:**
- As pedestrian, owner, holder, or user of bicycles or other non-motorized vehicles
- As passenger in any vehicle
- Electric mobility devices (e-bikes, speed pedelecs, hoverboards, segways, e-scooters, electric wheelchairs, monowheels) with maximum autonomous speed limited to 25 km/h
- Damage when driving motor or rail vehicles without required legal age, outside parents' knowledge
- Occasional driving of borrowed motor vehicles (if legally qualified and not stolen)
- Drones and remote-controlled model aircraft (if not subject to mandatory insurance)

**Buildings and Property:**
- Main residence, second residence, or vacation residence
- Buildings under construction or renovation intended as main or second residence
- Entire building used as main residence containing maximum 3 apartments and/or 3 rented garages
- Student accommodation (including furniture) temporarily rented outside main residence
- Buildings used for liberal profession or commercial activity without storage or sale of goods
- Well-maintained elevators owned or maintained by insured
- Gardens and land adjacent to insured buildings
- Parking spaces for personal use (whether adjacent to buildings or not)
- Hotel rooms or similar accommodations during temporary stays
- Other buildings (with premium surcharge)

**Fire, Explosion, and Smoke:**
Material damage from fire, explosion, or smoke originating from fire in:
- Hotels or similar lodgings during temporary or occasional stays (private or business)
- Other buildings or mobile homes temporarily/occasionally used for vacation, family celebrations, or private/business travel

**Special Coverage:**
- **Voluntary assistance to insured:** Damage suffered by third parties providing voluntary and selfless assistance to rescue insured persons or their property (maximum amount as specified in special conditions)
- **Missing children coverage:** Up to €20,000 (non-indexed) for:
  - Legal costs and fees during criminal investigation
  - Medical-psychological support costs for family members
  - Other search-related costs (excluding if family member involved)
  - Deductible: €125 (non-indexed)

### 2. Legal Assistance Coverage (Rechtsbijstand)

#### **Coverage Limits:**

**Criminal Defense:** Up to €25,000
- Defense costs for violations of traffic laws and regulations as rider, pedestrian, cyclist, or user of other non-motorized vehicles
- Defense for claims covered under the Civil Liability insurance

**Civil Recovery:** Up to €25,000
- Recovery of damage suffered where third party is liable on extra-contractual basis only
- Minimum threshold: €531.53 (indexed)

**Insolvency Coverage:** Up to €7,500
- Compensation when liable third party is proven insolvent

**Criminal Bail:** Up to €12,500
- Advance payment of criminal bail required by foreign authorities

#### **Covered Costs:**
- Investigation, expertise, consultation costs
- Legal fees and court costs
- Execution of enforceable title costs
- Necessary travel and accommodation expenses for mandatory court appearances abroad
- Petition for pardon or rehabilitation costs

## General Requirements

### **Who is Insured:**

**Primary Insured:**
- Policyholder and cohabiting spouse (with main residence in Belgium)

**Family Members (Gezinsleden):**
- Spouse or cohabiting partner
- All persons living with policyholder, including students studying away from home
- Children no longer living at home, provided they have not formed their own family and are financially supported
- Children under co-parenting arrangements, even when staying with non-insured parent

**Additional Covered Persons:**
- Household staff and domestic help in private service
- Persons (paid or unpaid) supervising children or insured pets
- Minors temporarily under supervision of insured or family members

**Special Provision:**
- In case of divorce: coverage continues for 1 year for persons losing "resident" status due to divorce

### **Eligibility Criteria:**
- Main residence must be in Belgium
- Coverage applies to private life activities only
- Volunteer work considered part of private life (subject to volunteer law provisions)

### **Territory:**
- Worldwide coverage

## Major Exclusions

### **What is NOT Covered:**

**General Exclusions:**
1. Liability arising from professional activities
2. Damage caused by riding horses owned by insured (except rented/borrowed)
3. Damage caused by wild animals (even tamed)
4. Damage from aircraft owned, rented, or used by insured
5. Damage from sailboats over 300 kg or motorboats over 11 HP owned, rented, or used by insured
6. Material damage from fire/explosion/smoke in owned/rented/occupied buildings (except temporary stays)
7. Damage during hunting activities
8. Damage to movable/immovable property and animals under insured's supervision
9. Liability subject to mandatory insurance by law (except specific exceptions)
10. Damage from war, civil war, or terrorism
11. Damage from nuclear reactions, radioactivity, and ionizing radiation
12. Damage from buildings not used as main residence (except student accommodations)
13. Damage from buildings during construction, reconstruction, or adaptation work

**Age-Related Exclusions:**
- Family members over 18 years causing damage through:
  - Intentional acts
  - Under influence of alcohol or drugs
  - During fights/brawls

**Vehicle Exclusions:**
- Electric vehicles (e-bikes, speed pedelecs, hoverboards, wheelchairs, segways) with autonomous speed exceeding 25 km/h without pedal assistance

**Legal Assistance Exclusions:**
1. Professional activity-related damage (except specific exceptions)
2. Buildings not used as main residence (except specified properties)
3. Motor vehicles, trailers, or caravans under mandatory insurance law
4. Material damage from fire in owned/rented/occupied buildings
5. Fights participated in by insured
6. Contractual liability
7. Hunting activities
8. Damage to non-household animals or owned riding horses
9. Aircraft, sailboats over 300 kg, motorboats over 11 HP
10. War, civil war, or terrorism-related damage

## Insured Obligations

### **At Contract Inception:**
- Truthfully declare all circumstances relevant to risk assessment
- Cannot conceal information insurer should reasonably know

### **During Contract:**
- Notify significant and permanent risk changes
- Report changes in civil status or family composition
- Take all reasonable measures to prevent accidents
- Maintain elevators in good condition (if applicable)

### **In Case of Claim:**
- Report claim in writing within 8 days (or as soon as reasonably possible)
- Provide all useful information and documents immediately
- Forward all judicial and extrajudicial documents immediately
- Appear at hearings and perform necessary procedural acts
- Refrain from:
  - Acknowledging liability
  - Making settlements
  - Making payments or promises of payment
  - (First aid or mere acknowledgment of facts not considered liability admission)
- Repay recovered legal costs, court costs, and expertise costs

### **Breach Consequences:**
- Fraudulent breach: coverage denial
- Other breaches: reduction or recovery of benefits proportional to insurer's prejudice
- Burden of proof on insurer

## Claims Procedure

### **Notification:**
- Within 8 days of claim (or when reasonably possible)
- In writing to insurer
- Include all relevant details and circumstances

### **Insurer's Role:**
- Defend insured within coverage limits
- Right to contest third-party claims
- May compensate claimant if appropriate
- Manage dispute within coverage scope
- No admission of liability on behalf of insured

### **Legal Assistance:**
- Free choice of lawyer/legal representative
- Insurer covers costs and fees per coverage limits
- In case of disagreement: lawyer consultation at insurer's expense
- In case of conflict of interest: free choice from amicable phase

### **Subrogation:**
- Insurer subrogated to insured's rights against responsible third parties
- Up to amount paid
- Partially compensated insured has priority for remaining amount

### **Right of Recourse:**
Insurer can recover from insured aged 16+ who caused claim through:
- Intentional acts
- Gross negligence: drunkenness, alcohol intoxication, drug influence, fighting

**Recovery Limits:**
- Up to €11,000: full recovery
- Over €11,000: €11,000 + 50% of amount exceeding €11,000
- Maximum recovery: €31,000 (net expenses)
- Fraud cases: minimum €100 administrative/legal costs
